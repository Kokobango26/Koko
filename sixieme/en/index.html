The restaurant

The French cuisine of Le Sixième

Convinced that “what you eat reflects who you are”, Chef Clément and his team serve dishes prepared with fresh, natural ingredients sourced, as far as possible, from local producers.

To bring out all their flavours, we cook on our remarkable “Josper” charcoal oven.

The French cuisine of Le Sixième will win your heart with its unique taste. The whole team does everything to make sure you have a delicious time. See you soon.

  • Fresh, natural produce from local producers
  • Josper charcoal oven
  • Air-conditioned dining room
